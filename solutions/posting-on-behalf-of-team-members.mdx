title: "Post On Behalf Of Team Members"
description: "Admins can post and schedule LinkedIn content using a team member's account, so content goes out under the individual's name and network rather than a company page. This is entirely opt-in. Each member controls whether admins can do this."
---

## How the opt-in works

Every team member with a connected LinkedIn account sees an **Admin Posting Permission** card on their **Settings → LinkedIn** tab. The toggle is **off by default**.

| Toggle state | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Off (default) | Admins cannot post or schedule on this member's behalf |
| On | Admins can select this member in the "Post As" picker |

Members can flip the toggle at any time. Turning it off immediately removes them from the admin picker. No new posts can be created for them. Already-queued scheduled posts are not retroactively cancelled (they will still fire).

> Admins are never subject to this toggle. They can always post as themselves.

## Admin workflow

### Posting now

1. Open any content card or the Content Preview page.
2. Click **Post to LinkedIn**.
3. If the org has opted-in members, a **Post As** selector appears. Choose yourself or any opted-in member.
4. Click **Post**.

### Scheduling for later

1. Click **Schedule for Later** on a content card or preview.
2. Select a date and time.
3. If applicable, choose who to post as from the **Post As** picker.
4. Confirm: The post appears in the Calendar for the selected member.

### Editing or rescheduling

Admins can edit or reschedule any `pending` or `failed` post across the whole organization from the **Calendar** page. The opt-in is not re-checked at reschedule time. It was verified when the post was originally created.
